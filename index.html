What is Sustainable Energy?

Sustainable energy is energy produced from resources that naturally replenish and can be used without permanently damaging the environment. The goal is to meet today's energy needs while protecting tomorrow's world.

WHY IT MATTERS

Benefits of clean energy.

Less Pollution

Renewable energy can significantly reduce air pollution and greenhouse-gas emissions compared with fossil fuels.

Renewable

Sunlight, wind and water are naturally replenished and can support long-term energy needs.

Energy Security

Local renewable resources can reduce dependence on imported fossil fuels.

Innovation

Clean-energy technology creates opportunities for smarter infrastructure and new solutions.
